# NimChess

Mobile chess club inside Nimiq Pay. Warm midnight green, butter yellow, ivory pieces. A sculpted amber knight mascot gives identity; readable vector pieces make the board practical. No desktop scaling on phones. Board is primary during games. Top header and safe-area bottom tabs: Play, Puzzles, Club, You.

Typography: Space Grotesk display and numbers, DM Sans body. Background #111713, surfaces #1b241e, accent #f4d35e, text #f7f4e9, muted #a5b0a5. Board ivory #e6ddc5 / sage #76927b. 44px targets, focus rings, reduced motion. Main shell max 1120 desktop; phone full width with 16px gutters, board edge-to-edge within available width. No fake users or metrics.

Complete release scope: bot matches with difficulty, legal moves/castling/en passant/promotion/endings; local pass-and-play; server-authoritative friend matches, share links, clocks, resign/draw/rematch, reconnect; daily and practice puzzles with hints/solutions/streaks; profile editing, avatar/theme settings, NimRank XP separate from multiplayer rating, history with PGN replay/export; real club leaderboard; official Nimiq Pay connect/disconnect and optional peer tips with explicit amount/network/recipient confirmation and honest provider state; how-to-play/facts and accessible interactions.

No paid competitive matches or prizes in this release: proposal placed tournaments after fair-play maturity. No fake wallet success or payment execution in tests. Production acceptance requires real phone provider testing by wallet owner.
